Print files

Send the shop a PDF. Use this page: Print → Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF). Turn off headers and footers, and do not scale — page size is already the card plus bleed.

Give the shop this spec

File
PDF, 31 pages (30 fronts, then 1 back). RGB is fine for digital print.
Page size
69 × 123.6 mm (includes 3 mm bleed on every side)
Trim to
63 × 117.6 mm — same proportions as the on-screen card
Bleed
3 mm. Photo runs to the page edge. Cut on the inner yellow/black frame.
Resolution
Export/print at 300 dpi or higher. Do not “fit to paper”.
Backs
Page 31 is the common back. Print it on the reverse of every front.
Stock
300–350 gsm card, optionally with a matte or linen laminate.
Finish
Rounded corners ~3 mm radius if they can match the screen cards.

This is a custom size, not a poker card (63 × 88 mm). If they can only print poker size, the art will be cropped or letterboxed — ask them to cut 63 × 118 mm instead.
