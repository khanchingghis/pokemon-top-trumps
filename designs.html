Six design options

Same three cards — Charizard, Blastoise, Gengar — with real scene photos behind the official art, and a different typeface plus layout each time. Reply with a number (1–6). Photos are type-matched (sun, surf, fog, lightning, aurora, nebula, and so on) so a full-deck pass would swap the background with the Pokémon’s primary type.

1 Cinematic poster

Sports-movie energy. Full-bleed photo, oversized sprite, giant Bebas Neue name, Oswald film-strip stats. Dark gradient keeps numbers readable. Most “exciting” at arm’s length.

2 Classic Top Trumps

Winning Moves yellow chevrons, cream stock, gold art window — but the window is a real photo instead of a white box. Name in Anton. The safest “this is still Top Trumps” option.

3 Comic smash

Bangers type, halftone dots, starburst, fat black borders, two-column smash numbers. Loud on the table; stats are the easiest to read of the photo treatments.

4 Pokédex terminal

Share Tech Mono, scanlines, dotted leaders, green-on-black HUD. Photo sits as a ghosted backdrop. Reads as a device, not a toy card.

5 Field dossier

Manila folder, Polaroid of the scene, Barlow Condensed ruled stats, red TYPE stamp. Scout-report energy — good if the game should feel like a field guide rather than a poster.

6 Stadium jersey

Black Ops One nameplate, giant faded card number, type-coloured banner, compact stat grid. Trading-card / FIFA-sticker energy. Photo still full-bleed.
